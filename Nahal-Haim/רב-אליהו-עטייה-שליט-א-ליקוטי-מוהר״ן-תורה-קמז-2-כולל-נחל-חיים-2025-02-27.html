Rather, his entire goal is only to strengthen another Jew for future generations in the service of God, another Jew, and another Jewish woman. And just as education helps, I will tell you a beautiful butterfly, and this is it. Humility will not give enough like flour. Someone is holding the head.

Well, Samuel will not give enough honest grapes. Flour. Flour. Flour.

Well, who is flour? So I read it like this. Don't give a grape. Dakna.

Nachman. Gematria. Flour. This is one of the most beautiful butterflies.
