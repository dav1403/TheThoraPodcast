The Torah tells us that when Moses   addresses the people of Israel, he   begins by saying "Hayom," you are all   standing here before me today. The   great, the not-so-great, children,   adults, the elderly, you name it, he   lists all the components of the people   of Israel and he is going to say a few   words to them. And we know that Moses,   before leaving this world, says many,   many things so that the people of   Israel remember the fundamentals and   what they must build their future   history upon. But what is very   interesting is what? It is noticing   that when Moses speaks to the people of   Israel, he tells them "You are all here  ." In other words, this concerns each   and every one of you. Everyone must   listen to these words. These words are   valid for everyone. Let us not exclude   ourselves. You know, I think one of the   tragedies today is when people say   religion is for this or that group,   forgetting that in truth, we are all   concerned. We do it a little less, a   little more, depending on the habits   formed or not, and the world in which   we grew up. But it is something that   concerns us all, and that is what Moses   is saying. You are all here, you are   all concerned, do not forget it.
