Rabbi Zusha of Anipoli said that when   he reaches Heaven, they won't ask him:   Why weren't you Moses our Teacher? Why   weren't you King David? Why weren't you   Rabbi Akiva? They will ask him: Why   weren't you Zusha? As we now approach   Rosh Hashanah, the day man was created,   every person must say, as the Sages   teach, "The world was created for my   sake." When the New Year arrives, one   must realize the importance of each and   every one of us and our mission to   bring the redemption. Every Jew is an   emissary of the Holy One, blessed be He  , to bring the complete redemption. And   how do we do that? Through Torah,   commandments, and good deeds. Always   remember that I am important, that each   of us has significance; the Holy One,   blessed be He, created him just like   Adam, and one small commandment brings   the redemption.
