Not only if you are happy at Soucotte, you will be happy throughout the year. The Outer rave, Paraditak, the Itur rave, he says that if someone is in joy at Sous-Cote, and more precisely at the moment of Araba which is the end, the end, the end, the end of Soucotte—the moment we have, you know, we are going to separate there, we have the end—it is he, the one who will know how to be in joy, who will know…I tell you, it is something that has to be worked on. For me, it is…it is not something that was intuitive to me. The one who tries to be in joy at Sous-Cote, at the moment of Araba will be in joy all year long. Why? Because all year long, no matter the situations you will be in, no matter the wanderings sometimes in which you will even feel blocked, you will feel only one thing, it is that Akad is with you. Yeah.
