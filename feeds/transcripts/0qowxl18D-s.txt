The president they had before, named Houphouët-Boigny, he was the president of Ivory Coast, and he kept the custom from Joseph the Righteous: every January 1st, he gave the tithe of Ivory Coast to Eretz Israel. Every January 1st, he kept the tithe of Joseph the Righteous. He was very meticulous about that. I said, well, that is why, that is why our Rebbe is here. Our Rebbe came here and one might ask, why this country? Why is Africa so big? An enormous continent, there are plenty of countries. Why did he come there? That is why he came there, because without fail, at the start of every year, the tithe of a country like Ivory Coast— it is not a poor country, so the tithe of this country is billions and billions every January 1st to Israel. There, as we are told.
